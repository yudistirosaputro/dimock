# Code of conduct

dimock is maintained by volunteers and used by people at work. Everyone interacting in the repository, issue tracker, and discussions is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep it professional: no harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention, anywhere the project is discussed.
- Stay on topic and make room for others, especially newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on scope; the PRD says what the project is and is not, and "no" is an acceptable answer to a feature request.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour go privately to the maintainer via the email on the GitHub profile of the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project's spaces, at the maintainer's discretion.
